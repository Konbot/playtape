Layout

  • Timeline on top, mixer docked below — collapse it with the MIXER bar when you just need to record.
  • Hit ● with nothing armed and TAPE creates + arms a track for you. Latency is compensated by rec offset (auto-estimated; use Calibrate for an exact measurement).
  • Tap a lane or the ruler to place the playhead. Ctrl+scroll (pinch on trackpads) zooms the timeline.
  • On browsers without folder access (phones, Firefox/Safari), Save downloads a single .tape file — Open or drag it back in to resume.

Editing clips

  • Click a clip to select it. Drag its body to move; drag its edges to trim.
  • Repeat drops a copy right after — great for looping a part.
  • Copy / Paste — paste lands at the playhead on the selected clip's track.
  • Cut a section: Split at the playhead twice (start & end of the bad bit), select the middle, Delete.

FX & output

  • Each strip's FX button opens its pedalboard (drive / cab / delay / tremolo), non-destructive.
  • Bounce = full mix with limiter — includes the drum machine and stylophone when their LIVE is on; Stems = each track separately.
  • If your project is saved as a folder, bounces & stems are written into that folder; otherwise they download.

Loop & punch-in

  • Drag across the ruler to set a loop region; LOOP (or L) turns it on/off. Playback cycles the region.
  • Hit ● with a loop on and TAPE punches in: it rolls in a bit early, but only the loop region lands on the track — retry a solo or a verse without touching the rest.

Autosave

  • Saved projects are autosaved every 45s. Unsaved sessions get a backup inside the browser — if TAPE closes unexpectedly, it offers to restore on next launch.

Shortcuts

  • Space play · R rec · L loop · Ctrl+C/V copy/paste · Ctrl+D repeat · S split · Delete remove clip · Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+S save
